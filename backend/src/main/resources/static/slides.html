The Solution

From Passive Ticket-Logging to Active Interception

Orchestrating neighborhood-level boreholes, JoJo tanks, and mobile bowsers in real-time.

Traditional Civic Ticketing

  • ✕ Reactive: Merely logs what already broke after widespread citizen complaints.
  • ✕ Siloed: Treats every complaint as an isolated ticket with zero spatial context.
  • ✕ Bottlenecked: 100% reliant on scarce municipal repair crews and water bowsers.
  • ✕ Passive: Sends generic SMS updates while institutions run dry.

The RippleReady Paradigm

  • ✓ Predictive: Computes downstream cascade risks before buffer runs out.
  • ✓ Interdependent: Maps crèches, clinics, and FNB merchants by urgency runway.
  • ✓ Horizontal: Unlocks private community boreholes, church JoJo tanks, and bakkies.
  • ✓ Actionable Rails: Real-time token handshake + automated FNB Instant Pay fuel stipends.
System Architecture

The 4-Stage Autonomous Engine

Deterministic, sub-second execution with end-to-end cryptographic accountability.

01

Verified Ingestion

Ward leader reports disruption with geocoordinates. The engine enforces boundaries and dedupes confirmations automatically.

Epic 1 • POST /api/incidents
02

Ripple Modeling

Evaluates 2.0 km perimeter. Sorts institutions by runway buffer into Tier 1 (Life), Tier 2 (Food), and Tier 3 (SME).

Epic 2 • 3ms Spatial Math
03

Mutual Aid Match

Matches nearest capacity within 3.5 km. Atomically locks volume, decrements reserve, and issues a 6-char OTP token.

Epic 3 • Atomic Locking
04

FNB Handshake

Recipient verifies physical delivery with token. Node turns GREEN, R150 fuel stipend is disbursed via FNB rails, SHA-256 logged.

⚡ FNB Instant Pay / eWallet Micro-Rails

Enables instant, transparent R150 fuel disbursements directly to volunteer bakkie drivers upon recipient token confirmation. Eliminates cumbersome municipal paperwork and provides corporate disaster funds with a fraud-proof delivery rail.

Sub-Second Payout • Single-Use Replay-Proof Tokens

🔐 Cryptographic Audit Ledger (SHA-256)

Every state change—from initial rupture to water delivery and payment—calculates a SHA-256 hash chained to the prior block. Guaranteed proof of delivery for ESG reporting, donor assurance, and internal compliance.

Immutable Block Hashes • Full Traceability
